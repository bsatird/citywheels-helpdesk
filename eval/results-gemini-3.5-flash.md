## How the score is computed

- `tools`: every tool in `expect_tools` was actually called.
- `amounts`: every expected euro amount appears in the answer (1 cent tolerance).
- `phrases`: every group of alternatives has at least one match (case-insensitive).
- `no-invention`: none of the `forbid_phrases` appears as a claim (a negated
  mention such as "as a voucher, not as cash" does not count).
- `refusal`: out-of-scope questions are declined instead of answered.
- `grounded`: every euro amount in the answer appears in some tool result.
- A case passes only when all of its checks pass.
